# Compliance: AI-Content Labeling

## EU AI Act, Article 50 (in force for transparency since 2 Aug 2026)

Photorealistic synthetic people in advertising are NOT covered by the artistic exemption — they require visible disclosure at first contact with the content.

Checklist for every campaign package:
- [ ] Every AI-generated visual asset carries or ships with a visible label (e.g., "AI-generated") or the official EU icon set ("Fully AI-Generated" / "Partially AI-Modified")
- [ ] Metadata/machine-readable marking where the format supports it (C2PA or equivalent)
- [ ] Instagram deliveries: remind the client that AI-persona accounts need the platform's "AI-generated profile" label (organic reach is limited without it since Aug 2026)
- [ ] Package states labeling responsibility split: creator delivers labeled masters; client keeps labels on republication

## Disclosure snippets (copy-paste)

Site/social caption (EN): `AI-generated campaign visuals. Independent concept — not affiliated with <brand>.`
Short label (EN): `AI-generated`
Client handoff note (EN): `All visuals are AI-generated and labeled per EU AI Act Art. 50. Please keep the label on all republications.`

## Concept-work disclaimer

Any package built on a real brand for portfolio/spec purposes must include: `Independent concept, not affiliated with or endorsed by <brand>.` Never imply the brand is a client.
