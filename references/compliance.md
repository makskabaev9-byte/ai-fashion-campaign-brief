# Compliance: AI-Content Labeling

Practical guidance for campaign packages. This is not legal advice: obligations depend on the
content, who publishes it and where. When in doubt, the client should check with their own counsel.

## EU AI Act, Article 50 (transparency obligations, applicable from 2 August 2026)

Article 50 splits duties between two roles:

- **Providers** of AI systems that generate images, video, audio or text must make outputs
  marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as AI-generated (Art. 50(2)).
  This mostly concerns the generation tools themselves.
- **Deployers** — whoever uses an AI system to publish content — must disclose that image,
  audio or video content is artificially generated or manipulated when it is a **deep fake**:
  content that resembles existing persons, objects, places or events and would falsely appear
  authentic (Art. 50(4)).
- For content that is **evidently artistic, creative, satirical or fictional**, the disclosure
  duty is limited to disclosing it in a way that does not hamper the display or enjoyment of the work.

What this means for AI fashion campaigns:

- Photorealistic AI models and scenes can read as real to the viewer. A clear "AI-generated"
  label is the safe default for advertising, even where the law might not strictly require it.
- Content that imitates a **real, identifiable person** (a celebrity, a founder, a known model)
  is the highest-risk case: label it clearly and get that person's written consent.
- Check the platform rules too: Meta, TikTok and YouTube have their own AI-content labels
  and may require them for realistic AI imagery.

## Checklist for every campaign package

- [ ] Every AI-generated visual ships with a visible label (e.g., "AI-generated") unless the client decides otherwise in writing
- [ ] Machine-readable marking (C2PA or equivalent) kept where the tool and format support it
- [ ] Platform AI labels applied when posting realistic AI imagery
- [ ] Written consent on file for any real person whose likeness is used
- [ ] Handoff note states the split: creator delivers labeled masters; client keeps labels on republication

## Disclosure snippets (copy-paste)

Site/social caption (EN): `AI-generated campaign visuals. Independent concept — not affiliated with <brand>.`
Short label (EN): `AI-generated`
Client handoff note (EN): `All visuals are AI-generated and delivered with an AI-content label. Please keep the label on all republications.`

## Concept-work disclaimer

Any package built on a real brand for portfolio/spec purposes must include: `Independent concept, not affiliated with or endorsed by <brand>.` Never imply the brand is a client.
