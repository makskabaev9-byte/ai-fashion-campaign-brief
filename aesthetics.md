# Aesthetic Directions

Choose ONE primary direction per campaign. Mixing more than one primary dilutes the concept.

## Classic directions (evergreen)

| Direction | Core look | Lighting | Lens language | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial Minimal | Negative space, muted neutrals, one accent | Soft window light, high key | 50–85mm, shallow DOF | Skincare, fragrance, premium basics |
| Studio Flash | Hard direct flash, raw background | On-camera flash, hard shadows | 35mm, snapshot energy | Streetwear, youth beauty |
| Golden Hour Glow | Warm backlit skin, haze | Backlit sun, lens flare | 85mm wide open | Suncare, summer fragrance, haircare |
| Night City | Neon reflections, wet asphalt, motion blur | Practical neons + street light | 28–35mm, handheld motion | Fragrance, nightlife fashion, energy drinks |
| Macro Texture | Product/surface as landscape | Raking light, focus stacking | Macro 100mm | Cosmetics textures, food-beauty, ASMR cuts |
| Archive Editorial | 90s/00s print ad pastiche, visible grain | Mixed tungsten/daylight | 35mm film look | Fashion throwback capsules |
| Clean E-com | Seamless background, floating product | Perfect softbox, no shadows | 70mm, locked off | Conversion assets, marketplaces |

## 2026 trend aesthetics (fast-moving; re-verify before pitching)

| Trend | Signal | One-line concept angle |
|---|---|---|
| Glitchy Glam | Deliberate asymmetry: mismatched nails, two-tone lips | "Perfectly imperfect" — controlled asymmetry only AI can repeat exactly |
| Vamp Romantic | Dark romance, wet lids, oxblood lips | Gothic romance without a gothic location budget |
| Extra Celestial | Opalescence, lunar dust, alien-core | Impossible light sources and floating particulate |
| Glamoratti | 80s power luxury, big shoulders, gold | Full 80s campaign with zero costume/prop rental |
| Gimme Gummy | Jelly/ASMR textures, rubber-look gloss | Macro ASMR loops of impossible product textures |
| Scent Stacking | Layered niche fragrance visuals | "What a layered scent looks like" — visualizing the invisible |

## Justification rule

When presenting the chosen direction, name: (1) the brand signal it matches, (2) the platform behavior it exploits (e.g., "macro texture wins the first frame on Reels"), (3) what it would cost as a physical shoot. One line each.
